# Earth textures

All three images are NASA imagery (public domain), resized and converted to WebP for the web.

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `earth-day.webp` | NASA Earth Observatory — Blue Marble Next Generation, July 2004 (`world.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg`), resized to 4096×2048 |
| `earth-night.webp` | NASA Earth Observatory — Black Marble 2016 (`BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg`), 3600×1800 |
| `earth-clouds.webp` | NASA Earth Observatory — Blue Marble clouds (`cloud_combined_2048.jpg`), 2048×1024, greyscale |

https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov — NASA imagery is not copyrighted; credit "NASA" is appreciated.
